NVMe, SATA, HDD—and the drives Windows can detect.

The app combines Windows storage information with smartmontools SMART readings. It includes internal and external storage where the drive and controller expose the relevant data.

  • NVMe SSDs, including many M.2 drives.
  • SATA SSDs in 2.5-inch or M.2 form factors.
  • Mechanical hard drives, with hours and health where available.
  • USB, SAS/SCSI, and RAID storage, depending on SMART pass-through support.

M.2 is a form factor. It does not automatically mean NVMe. Administrator access may be required for SMART readings.

No drive-health tool can predict every failure.

Remaining endurance estimates the write-wear budget reported by an SSD. It does not tell you how many days or years the drive will last. HDD remaining life is shown as N/A, and unsupported counters stay Unknown. Keep backups even when SMART reports a pass.

Questions about checking SSD health

How do I check how much life is left in my SSD?

Download and open MaxLab Dynamics SSD Life Free, then look at the SSD's endurance column. When the drive reports percentage used, the app shows 100 minus that figure, floored at zero. This is remaining write endurance, not a guaranteed lifespan.

Why are power-on hours or remaining endurance Unknown?

Some drives, USB adapters, RAID controllers, and firmware do not expose every SMART counter. Try administrator mode and inspect the scan notes. The app keeps a value Unknown instead of guessing.

Can it show an HDD's remaining life?

It can show reported HDD health, power-on hours, temperature, and available SMART attributes. There is no reliable universal remaining-life percentage for HDDs, so that field is N/A.

Does the app upload my drive information?

Drive scanning works locally and offline. Reports are saved only when you choose Export. They may include drive serial numbers and labels, so review a report before sharing it. Clicking the website link opens your browser.

Is MaxLab Dynamics SSD Life Free actually free?

Yes. Version 1.0.0 is a free portable download, with application source code under the MIT license. The bundled smartmontools reader has its own GPL license and matching source, included in the package.
